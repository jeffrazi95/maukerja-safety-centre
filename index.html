Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about hiring source checks and staying safe.

How does Hiring Source Verification work?

If you've received a hiring message, call or email related to a job you applied for through Maukerja, share the details with us. We'll check the information against Maukerja's job and employer records, and confirm directly with the employer when needed, then let you know whether the hiring source can be verified.

What information should I provide for verification?

Provide the company name, job position, phone number or email that contacted you, and a screenshot of the hiring message or interview invitation.

Can Maukerja check a phone number or email that contacted me?

Yes. Share the phone number or email that contacted you together with the company name, job position and relevant screenshots, and we'll help verify the hiring source.

What does "Unable to Verify" mean?

It means we couldn't associate the hiring contact with the employer using the information available to us. It doesn't confirm anything either way — we'd recommend being cautious, and avoiding sharing OTPs, making payments or providing sensitive information until you're confident about the hiring source.

How long does Hiring Source Verification take?

Some cases can be checked using the information available to us. Others may take longer if we need to confirm the details directly with the employer.

Can Maukerja check a hiring message for a job I applied for somewhere else?

Hiring Source Verification is for hiring messages related to jobs you've applied for through Maukerja. If the message is unrelated to a Maukerja application, we may not have the information needed to verify the hiring source.

What if I don't remember applying for the job?

Check your Maukerja application history first, and avoid sharing sensitive information or making any payment while you're unsure. If the hiring message is related to a job you applied for through Maukerja, you can submit the details for Hiring Source Verification.

Will Maukerja share the employer's registered phone number or email?

No. For privacy and security purposes, we don't disclose an employer's registered contact information. Share the contact details you received with us and we'll help verify the hiring source.

What should I do while waiting for verification?

Avoid sharing OTPs, making payments or providing sensitive personal information until you're confident about the hiring source.

What should I do if something about a hiring message doesn't feel right?

Pause before you respond. Avoid making payments, sharing OTPs or providing sensitive personal information, and keep screenshots or details of the message. You can also share what you received with us for Hiring Source Verification.
